# Attribution

ScreenStride includes a Swift adaptation of protocol implementations and
reverse-engineering findings from iShareScreen:

- Upstream: https://github.com/renegadelink/iShareScreen
- Authors: iShareScreen contributors
- Pinned revision: 9ab40d3a3151524f954cff9d6239d891197c1705
- License: GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or any later version

Adapted areas include RSA/SRP authentication, enc1103 record encryption,
media offer serialization, RTP/RTCP key derivation and transport, and Apple's
H.264/HEVC packetization and AAC-ELD audio negotiation/decoding. Native Swift implementation and iPad integration were
created in September 2026. Reference fixture generation imports the pinned
upstream implementation.

The applicable AGPL license text is in LICENSE. Source for the app is in App,
Sources, project.yml and Package.swift. The reference checkout is retained
locally under research/iShareScreen and is excluded from the outer project's
source tracking; its pinned upstream source remains available at the URL above.

BigInt, by Károly Lőrentey and contributors, is distributed under the MIT
license: https://github.com/attaswift/BigInt. A copy of that license is in
THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/BigInt.txt.

System frameworks (CommonCrypto, CryptoKit, Security, VideoToolbox,
CoreMedia, CoreVideo, AVFoundation, SwiftUI and UIKit) are supplied by Apple.
