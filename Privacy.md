# Privacy policy

Effective September 12, 2026. Applies to ScreenStride and ScreenStride Gesture Helper.

The iPad client connects to the Mac address you choose. Screen pixels and Mac
audio are streamed to the iPad; touch, pointer and keyboard input are sent to
that Mac. No developer-operated relay, account service, advertising or analytics
service receives that session. The app does not record your screen or audio.

The Mac address, username and preferences are stored locally on the iPad.
When Remember password is enabled, your password is stored in this iPad's
Keychain with device-only protection and is accessible only while unlocked.
Use Forget saved password in the connection screen to remove that saved login.
Disabling Remember password removes the saved login for the selected Mac/account.

Connection diagnostics contain technical status and error messages. Passwords,
authentication keys, screen contents and audio contents are not logged. A
support report that you choose to share may reveal the address of your Mac;
review it before sharing. The app does not upload diagnostics automatically.

The optional ScreenStride Gesture Helper needs Accessibility permission to translate
three-finger gesture events received through Screen Sharing into desktop
movement. It does not listen on a network port, read your login password,
record keyboard contents, record screen/audio, or send data to the developer.
It stores its enabled preference locally; launch at login is a separate,
optional system setting. Quit the helper or revoke its Accessibility permission
to stop gesture translation.

Opening a support or GitHub download link leaves the app and is subject to the
website's privacy practices. This policy does not describe Apple's own operating
system, App Store or Screen Sharing diagnostics.
